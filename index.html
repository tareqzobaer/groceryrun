Parsing rules

One item per line. Quantity is parsed automatically from these formats:

2 applesnumber before name
apples x 2× or x after name
2x applesnumber + x prefix
apples (2)number in brackets
apples - 2dash or colon separator
apples 2number at end
applesno number → qty 1

Delimiters

Items can be separated by a new line or a comma.

Auto-merge

Adding an item that already exists (e.g. apple when Apple is in the list) bumps its quantity instead of creating a duplicate. Plurals are normalized: apples merges with apple.

Prefixes stripped

Leading bullets, dashes, and numbered list prefixes like 1. or 1) are removed automatically.
